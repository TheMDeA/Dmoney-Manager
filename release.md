## [2.5.0] - 2026-10-08

### Added
- Scanning a receipt now reads it with on-device text recognition and pre-fills the amount, merchant, and date — just verify and save.
- The Wallets page is now a card stack: tap a wallet to bring it forward with quick actions (Transfer, Top up, History) and its recent transactions.
- Wallets can now have a logo: pick from Indonesian bank and e-wallet templates (BCA, BRI, Mandiri, GoPay, DANA, OVO, ShopeePay, and more) — picking one also applies its brand color, which you can still change.

### Fixed
- Fixed the app slowing down or crashing when opening All Transactions with a large history: the list now only builds visible rows, and only newly added transactions animate.
