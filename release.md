## [2.7.2] - 2026-10-26

### Added

- Paylater dashboard now shows how much you've repaid this month, next to what you spent.
- Tap an installment plan to see its full history: the purchase and every payment.

### Fixed

- Paylater plans now record their purchase, so the amount owed, limit used and provider totals stay in sync with your plans instead of staying at zero.
- Home total now matches the Wallets screen — paylater balances are no longer counted in it, so paying an installment visibly lowers the total.
- Deleting an installment plan now removes its purchase and payments too, and restores wallet balances.
