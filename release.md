## [2.7.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added

- Paylater wallets: add a paylater account to track what you owe, when it's due, and how much of your limit you've used, with due-date reminders and installment plans, all from a new dashboard on the Wallets screen.
- Splitting a paylater purchase across 1, 3, 6, or 12 months now shows the monthly amount and total interest before you save it.
- A freeze switch blocks new paylater spending until you turn it back off.
- Bank and e-wallet accounts can now carry an annual interest rate, and the wallet detail shows a growth projection — estimated balance in 6 and 12 months plus a 12-month curve.
